ABOUT

Koppe is an electronic musician and sound artist exploring the space between deep club music, hypnotic dub, airy breaks, and atmospheric experimentation. His work is shaped by texture, pressure, and the shifting relationship between rhythm and space.

With roots in extreme music, his approach retains an underlying sense of intensity, even in its quieter moments. Weighty low frequencies, fragmented percussion, and slowly evolving atmospheres move between tension and release, density and stillness.

Following earlier work under the name White Sleep, Koppe continues to explore new rhythmic and sonic territory, drawing on the physicality of club music and the spaciousness of ambient sound.

Beyond recorded music, his practice extends into sound design for moving image, audiovisual experimentation, live coding, and audio programming. His interests also encompass music and interactive audio for video games and immersive VR environments. Through these disciplines, he experiments with creative technologies and explores the relationship between sound, image, space, and systems.
